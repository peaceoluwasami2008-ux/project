About-US

I provide a range of beginner-to-intermediate web development services
focused on creating clean, functional, and user-friendly websites. I can
help with the development of personal portfolio websites, landing pages,
simple business websites, and other basic web projects designed to
establish a strong online presence. Using technologies such as HTML, CSS;
and JavaScript, I can create responsive web pages that work effectively
across different screen sizes and devices. I can also help improve
existing websites by making changes to their layout, styling, navigation,
buttons, links, and other interactive elements. In addition to building
websites from scratch, I am interested in helping clients turn their ideas
into simple digital solutions by understanding their requirements and
developing a website around their goals. I pay attention to structure,
appearance, usability, and functionality so that the final website is both
visually appealing and easy to navigate. As I continue to grow as a
software engineering student and developer, I am constantly learning new
technologies and improving my ability to create more advanced and
effective solutions. Whether you need a simple portfolio, a landing page,
a basic business website, or assistance with an existing web project, I am
ready to learn about your requirements and work toward creating a solution
that meets your needs.
